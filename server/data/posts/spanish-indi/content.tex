
Ok, here we go with something i like, something i discovered when i was living
in Spain, ``la escena musical indie española''.  I knew some Spanish music, but
when i moved to Spain and i started to listen
\href{https://cariniopop.com}{Cariño}, my mind was blown. I started to listen
more and more Spanish music from the same production company,
\href{https://elefant.com/en/index}{Elefant Records}.

I remember that was the COVID time, and the first time i could see Cariño live
was in small presentation in Aranjuez (a small city near Madrid). I started to
follow more and more Spanish indie music, particuarly at that time i'd say that
was the bum of the Spanish indie music. \href{https://cariniopop.com}{Cariño},
\href{https://vananarecords.com/artist/ginebras/}{Ginebras},
\href{https://elefant.com/grupos/axolotes-mexicanos/biografia}{Axolotes
Mexicanos}, between others, were just at the bum of their career. I know, there
were some other bands before, like
\href{https://elefant.com/bands/papa-topo}{Papa Topo} or
\href{https://elefant.com/bands/la-casa-azul}{La Casa Azul} (the ones who
started Elefant Records), but i think many bands started to be more popular at
that time.

Still know there're many other bands and artist,
\href{https://whus.org/2023/03/meet-las-dianas-the-pop-punk-group-from-granada/}{Las
Dianas}, \href{https://russianred.com}{Russian Red} or
\href{https://elefant.com/grupos/lisasinson}{Lisa Sinson},
\href{https://elefant.com/grupos/pipiolas}{Pipiolas},
\href{https://elefant.com/grupos/aiko-el-grupo}{Aiko el Grupo},
\href{https://www.subterfuge.com/artistas/jordana-b/}{Jordana B},
\href{https://elefant.com/grupos/camera-obscura}{Camera Obscura},
\href{https://soundsfromspain.com/artista/marlena/?lang=en}{Marlena},
\href{https://elefant.com/grupos/cola-jet-set}{Cola Jet Set}, just to name a
few. 

It's quite ineresting to see how the Spanish music is evolving and how the
lyrics change, from the first steps of the Spanish (pop/indie/ne) music with
Los Bravos and Fórmula V to this new era, but what can i add?, ig there's no
better analysis for that times than the one given by Victor from Music Radar
Clan in this \href{https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pODdVTfWOrQ}{video}.
